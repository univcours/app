# NovaGrid Fireworks Celebration — Sprint 2

**Roles:** Game Designer (reward fairness), Godot Engineer (fireworks integration), Visual FX (sky rockets + spark-letter rain), Audio (four original synthesised explosions), Android Performance (strict particle caps), QA (Godot/Android regression).

## Gameplay
- 1 cleared group (real row/column/3x3 sector): a small fireworks rocket and glowing shower.
- 2 groups: twin sky fireworks and stronger original sound.
- 3 groups: multicolor Mega Fireworks and larger score/XP from the pre-existing genuine-clears scoring engine.
- 4+ groups: five-rocket Legendary Fireworks, player's name **written with luminous fragments**, then letters break into falling showers. In Low Effects mode the show uses fewer rockets and spark fragments.
- Every particle is purely visual. **No fictitious points, XP, bombs, stars or purchased rewards.** Multi-clear points continue to scale from the real Puzzle/Falling rules.
- Original rocket whistle, firecracker crackle and soft impact sound effects are generated by Audio.gd without external files or licensed sounds.
- Settings adds **PLAYER: NAME** (max 16 characters). Tap, enter name and SAVE NAME. Stored in SaveStore. Uses a particle-font alphabet for Latin letters and digits; names in other scripts (including Arabic) are kept intact and rendered using native fallback text with surrounding multicolour fireworks.
- Full effects cap 180 radial particles; reduced effects cap 48 and maximum two sky rockets; name fragments cap 270 (reduced: 72). All effects expire after five seconds, no ad or touch interception.
