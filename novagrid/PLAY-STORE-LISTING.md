## English (recommended default)

Name: NovaGrid

Short description:
Block puzzles, daily challenges and falling-block levels in one cosmic game.

Full description:
Discover NovaGrid, a colorful block puzzle game with different ways to play.

Classic: fit shapes on a 9×9 board and complete rows, columns and 3×3 squares to clear space.

Daily Challenge: return for a new puzzle and challenge your personal best.

Galaxy Stages: progress through seven different game sections and 14,000 varied stages with unique challenges and rewards.

Blockfall: control falling shapes with swipes, tap to rotate, and complete rows to score points.

Collect stars and in-game Nova Points when you complete stages, and unlock more challenges. Nova Points are gameplay rewards only, not real money.

Core puzzle gameplay works offline. Optional interstitial ads may require an internet connection and appear at natural breaks. There are no banner ads.

## Français

Découvrez NovaGrid, un jeu de réflexion avec plusieurs modes de puzzles de blocs.

Classique : placez des pièces sur une grille 9×9 pour compléter les lignes, les colonnes et les carrés 3×3.

Niveaux galactiques : explorez sept sections et 14 000 niveaux aux objectifs variés.
