Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

NovaGrid is an Android puzzle game with Classic, Daily Challenge, Galaxy Stages and Blockfall modes. You do not need an account to play.

Local game data

The game saves progress, scores, preferred language, settings and completed levels on your device in local application storage. The puzzle engine does not upload this game progress to a NovaGrid account or game server.

Advertising and data processed by others

The Android app integrates Google AdMob and the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP). Banner ads are disabled; the app may show occasional interstitial ads between gameplay sessions. Google's advertising services may process advertising identifiers, device information, approximate location inferred from your IP address, ad impressions and interactions, diagnostic information and consent choices in accordance with their policies and applicable law.

Internet and consent

Core puzzle gameplay works offline. Advertising and consent services may need an internet connection. Where required by law, Google's consent choices are provided. Gameplay is usable without showing ads.

Your options

Where required, the game offers Google's advertising privacy options. You may clear locally stored game information using Android Settings → Apps → NovaGrid → Storage → Clear storage, or by uninstalling the app.
